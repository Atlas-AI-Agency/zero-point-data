FAQ

Know the Context. Execute the Move.

How is the signal delivered?

The read is delivered daily through the dashboard and via real-time alerts. Each read includes the active regime, the BTC/Cash allocation stance, and the full factor breakdown driving the signal — alongside biweekly expert analysis.

Can I unsubscribe at any time?

Yes. Billing is quarterly and you can cancel renewal at any time — you retain access through the end of your current billing period.

Can I access the data via API?

Yes — and it's a core part of what we sell, not an afterthought. The regime score, allocation, and full factor series are available via our API (/api/signal) so you can automate directly against the read. Raw data exports (CSV/Parquet) are included too.

Why should I trust the edge?

Because you don't have to take our word for it. We publish the index construction, ship every signal with its driver attribution, and let you download the full historical signal log to audit the track record yourself. Transparency is the product.
